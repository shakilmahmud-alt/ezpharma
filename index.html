One platform. Every pharmacy you operate.

Tenant-isolated data, subscription billing, and AI tools — purpose-built for groups running multiple pharmacies.

Multi-tenant by design

Every pharmacy has its own fully isolated database, staff roles, and store settings. Data never leaks from one store to another.

Automated subscriptions, built-in

Pharmacies pay monthly or yearly via integrated payment gateways. Platform admin receives revenue directly with auto-invoicing.

Role & access control

Super admin for the platform, pharmacy admin for each store, and staff access for counter clerks. Fine-grained permissions keep your team on track.

AI prescription processing

Scan prescriptions to auto-populate patient records, drug dosages, and check for contraindications in seconds.

Drug interaction checker

Built-in safety warnings alert staff to lethal drug interactions and duplicate therapies before dispensing.

Smart inventory & forecasts

Never run out of critical meds. Automatic reorder recommendations based on historical sales and seasonal trends.

Expiry & batch management

Track batch numbers and expiry dates effortlessly. First-in, first-out (FIFO) alerts keep your dispensary compliant.

Analytics & reports

Revenue, gross margin, top-selling medications, staff performance, and more. Export to CSV or PDF instantly.

Pharmacy-level settings

Custom currency, receipt headers, tax rates, notification templates, and loyalty programs — configured individually per branch.
