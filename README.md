# EZ Pharma — Multi-Tenant Pharmacy Management SaaS Platform

EZ Pharma is a comprehensive, production-grade Multi-Tenant Pharmacy SaaS platform with Point of Sale (POS), smart inventory management, batch tracking, supplier & purchase order management, financial reporting & cash drawer auditing, customer management with loyalty tiers, notifications, and Super Admin platform management.

## 🚀 Features

- **Multi-Tenant Pharmacy Dashboard**: Individual portals for pharmacy branches.
- **Smart Point of Sale (POS)**: Real-time billing, split payments, cash drawer reconciliation, discounts & invoice printing.
- **Inventory & Batch Tracking**: Stock tracking, low-stock alerts, expiration dates, manufacturers & bulk Excel/CSV import.
- **Financial & Expense Management**: Daily sales breakdown, operating expenses, tax calculations, profit & loss, printable PDF/Excel reports.
- **Customer CRM**: Customer history, due balance collection, loyalty tiers & benefits.
- **Procurement & Purchase Orders**: Supplier directory, purchase orders creation & stock receiving workflows.
- **Notifications System**: Real-time stock alerts and system events.
- **Super Admin Platform Console**: Full control over tenant subscriptions, payment recordings, tenant activations/suspensions & pricing packages.
- **Modern UI**: Full Dark/Light mode support, responsive design, glassmorphic accents & micro-animations.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Vanilla CSS3 Design System.
- **Backend**: PHP RESTful APIs.
- **Database**: MySQL (`holidaym_ezpharma`).
