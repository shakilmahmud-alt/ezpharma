Customer Membership Tiers & Rules

Automatic loyalty tier progression rules and exclusive pharmacy benefits

Silver

Neighborhood Member

Qualification Rule: Local area-r je kono manush apnar pharmacy-te phone number diye register korlei ei tier-e jabe.

Exclusive Privileges:

  • Standard 2-3% discount on medicines.
  • SMS update on new health & wellness products.
  • Digital invoice & prescription record tracking.
Gold

Regular Patient

Qualification Rule: Jara niyamito masher oushudh (pressure, diabetes, kidney) kine. Monthly minimum 1,500 - 2,000 takar purchase korle ei tier pabe.

Exclusive Privileges:

  • Flat 5% discount on all purchases.
  • Free Blood Pressure (BP) Checkup on every visit.
  • Priority stock reservation before emergency runs out.
Platinum

Family Member

Qualification Rule: Jara pura poribarer oushudh, baby care products ba cosmetics ek sathe kine. Monthly 4,000 - 5,000 takar opore purchase korle tara Platinum hobe.

Exclusive Privileges:

  • Maximum 8-10% privilege discount.
  • Free Emergency Home Drop-off (within 1-2 km) for children & elderly.
  • Monthly Free Blood Sugar Test at pharmacy.
